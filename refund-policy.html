The Policy

Straightforward, line by line

01

Eligibility Terms

If the item arrives damaged, materially different from listing photos, or fails to arrive within the stated delivery window — you are 100% eligible. Open a request within 14 days of delivery.

02

Escrow Lock Clock

Your payment sits safely in the escrow vault until you click "Confirm Receipt". Sellers never receive funds prematurely before buyer verification occurs.

03

Simple 3-Step Process

Go to Dashboard → Select Order → Click "Get Help". Attach a quick photo or description. Our Trust & Safety desk reviews and issues decisions within 24h–48h.

04

Non-Refundable Scenarios

Custom bespoke items already in physical production, fully delivered digital goods, or orders confirmed delivered via GPS tracking with no photo evidence of defect.

05

Partial Refund Agreements

For minor cosmetic issues (e.g. minor box dent), buyer and seller can agree to a partial escrow payout. You keep the product while receiving a cash refund portion.

06

Chargeback Protection

Because funds are protected in escrow first, disputes resolve without bank delays. Pi crypto refunds settle in seconds; PayPal & Naira in 1–3 days.

The Refund Journey

How your money comes home

1 · You Open a Case

Pick the order, choose the issue, and attach photo evidence. Takes less than 2 minutes in your dashboard.

2 · Escrow Lock Activated

The seller's payout freezes instantly. The merchant is notified and given 24 hours to respond or assist.

3 · Human Review & Ruling

Trust & Safety reviews evidence from both sides. Clear claims are auto-approved in the buyer's favor.

4 · Payout Disbursement

Refunds land on your Pi Wallet in seconds, or return to your PayPal account/bank card in 1–3 business days.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast are refunds credited?

Most dispute cases are reviewed within 24 to 48 hours. Once approved, Pi Network crypto refunds settle into your wallet instantly. PayPal and Naira card refunds post in 1 to 3 business days.

What if the seller doesn't reply?

If a merchant fails to respond within 24 hours of a dispute lock, the escrow system automatically rules in your favor and releases a full refund to your payment method.

Can I request a partial refund?

Yes! For minor flaws like missing accessories or slight color mismatches, you can propose a partial refund amount inside your dispute portal while keeping the item.

Are Pi Network transactions reversible?

While blockchain transactions are final once executed, DropHub holds Pi payments inside an off-chain escrow smart vault during delivery, making full reversals 100% possible before order release.

Need to start a refund or open a case?

Open a claim directly in your buyer dashboard or talk to our Trust & Safety team — real humans reply within 4 hours.
