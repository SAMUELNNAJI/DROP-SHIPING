The payout flow

How payouts work

Every sale on DropHub is escrow-protected. Here’s the journey your money takes after a buyer hits checkout.

1

Buyer pays into escrow

When a buyer checks out, their payment — in Pi, PayPal or Naira — is held securely by DropHub Escrow. It’s not sent to you yet, and that’s a good thing: buyers purchase with confidence knowing their money is protected until delivery.

2

You ship, buyer confirms

Ship the order and add tracking from your Seller Dashboard. When the buyer confirms delivery, the order is marked complete. If the buyer doesn’t respond, orders auto-confirm 7 days after tracked delivery.

3

Payout triggers automatically

The moment escrow releases, your payout is queued automatically — no manual request, no minimum balance to chase, no waiting for a monthly payday. You’ll see it move to “In transit” in your dashboard instantly.

4

Funds land in your wallet

Pi payouts settle to your Pi wallet in seconds. PayPal disbursements arrive within 24 hours. Naira payouts post to your Nigerian bank account in 1–2 business days. Then you sell again.

Payout speed

When your money arrives

Settlement time depends on the rail the buyer paid with. Pick the payout method that suits your business.

Pi Network — Instant

Pi payments settle on-chain in seconds the moment escrow releases. Zero platform fees, no bank details needed — funds go straight to your registered Pi wallet address.

PayPal — Within 24 hours

PayPal payouts are disbursed within 24 hours of escrow release, straight to the PayPal email on your Seller Dashboard. Buyer-protection coverage applies to every order.

Naira (Paystack) — 1–2 days

Naira payouts are sent via Paystack to your Nigerian bank account within 1–2 business days of escrow release. Ideal for sellers sourcing and shipping locally.
