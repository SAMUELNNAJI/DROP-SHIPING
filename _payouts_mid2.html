Payout FAQ

Payout questions, answered

Everything sellers ask about getting paid on DropHub.

Payouts are triggered the moment a buyer confirms the order — typically within 24 hours of delivery. Pi payouts are instant on release; PayPal and bank payouts take 1–2 business days to land.
The escrow payout freezes instantly and you get 24 hours to respond with evidence — tracking, photos or messages. Our resolution team reviews and releases the funds if the claim is resolved in your favour. Disputes affect less than 1% of orders.
No minimum for Pi payouts — every sale pays out in full. PayPal and Naira payouts have a small $10 (or naira equivalent) minimum so transfer costs don’t eat into small orders. Anything below it simply accumulates in your balance until you cross it.
DropHub charges zero platform fees on payouts — you keep 100% of your escrow release. Pi payouts are completely free. PayPal and bank transfers may charge a small network or processing fee set by the payment provider, not by us.
Yes — go to Seller Dashboard → Payouts and switch between Pi wallet, PayPal or Naira at any time. Changes apply to all future payouts; anything already “in transit” continues to the original destination.
Each order pays out in the currency the buyer checked out with — Pi, PayPal (USD and more) or Naira. You can register multiple payout destinations and route each currency to the wallet or account you prefer.
