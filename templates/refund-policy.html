The Policy

Straightforward, line by line

Everything you need to know — no legal jargon.

01 · Eligibility

Eligibility Terms

If the item arrives damaged, materially different from listing photos, or fails to arrive within the stated delivery window — you are 100% eligible. Open a request within 14 days of delivery.

02 · Escrow

Escrow Lock Clock

Your payment sits safely in the escrow vault until you click "Confirm Receipt". Sellers never receive funds prematurely before buyer verification occurs.

03 · Process

Simple 3-Step Process

Dashboard → Select Order → Click "Get Help". Attach a photo or description. Trust & Safety reviews and issues a decision within 24–48 hours.

04 · Exceptions

Non-Refundable Scenarios

Custom bespoke items already in production, fully delivered digital goods, or GPS-confirmed deliveries with no photo evidence of defect are not refundable.

05 · Partial

Partial Refund Agreements

For minor cosmetic issues like a box dent, buyer and seller can agree on a partial escrow payout. Keep the product while receiving a cash refund portion.

06 · Chargeback

Chargeback Protection

Funds are held in escrow first, so disputes resolve without bank delays. Pi refunds settle in seconds; PayPal & Naira in 1–3 days.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast are refunds credited?
Most cases are reviewed within 24–48 hours. Pi Network refunds settle into your wallet instantly. PayPal and Naira card refunds post in 1–3 business days.
What if the seller doesn't reply?
If a merchant fails to respond within 24 hours of a dispute lock, the escrow system automatically rules in your favor and releases a full refund to your payment method.
Can I request a partial refund?
Yes. For minor flaws like missing accessories or slight color mismatches, you can propose a partial refund amount inside your dispute portal while keeping the item.
Are Pi Network transactions reversible?
While blockchain transactions are final once executed, DropHub holds Pi payments inside an off-chain escrow smart vault during delivery — making full reversals 100% possible before order release.
What evidence do I need to submit?
A clear photo or a 5-second video of the issue is sufficient for most cases. For non-delivery claims, we cross-check tracking data automatically — no extra steps needed.
Can a seller dispute my refund request?
Yes. Both parties can submit evidence. Our Trust & Safety team acts as a neutral arbiter and makes a binding decision within 48 hours based on the evidence provided.
