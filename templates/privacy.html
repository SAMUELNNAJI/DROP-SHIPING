The short version

DropHub does not sell personal data. We use information to create accounts, process orders, protect the marketplace, and communicate about the service. This policy explains the details without burying the important part.

01 · Information

What we collect

01

Account details

Name, email, password hash, optional phone number, and security settings so you can sign in safely.

02

Order details

Shipping information, order history, payment tokens, escrow status, and fulfillment details needed to complete a purchase.

03

Technical details

Device and browser information, approximate location, and essential activity logs used for reliability and fraud prevention.

02 · Purpose

How we use it

To run the marketplace

  • Create and secure your account
  • Process payments and deliver orders
  • Send receipts, service alerts, and tracking updates
  • Provide support and resolve disputes

To keep it trustworthy

  • Detect fraud, abuse, and suspicious activity
  • Improve the product using aggregated patterns
  • Meet tax, consumer protection, and legal duties
  • Protect the rights of buyers, sellers, and DropHub
03 · Boundaries

When we share information

We share only what is necessary with service providers who help us operate DropHub, such as payment processors, shipping partners, hosting providers, and security services. They are required to use the information for the agreed service, not their own advertising.

Our promise: We do not sell your information, use it for cross-site ad tracking, or share your contact details with sellers beyond what is needed to fulfill an order.
04 · Your rights

You stay in control

Access and export

Ask for a copy of your account, order, and wallet history in a portable format.

Correction

Update inaccurate profile or shipping information from your dashboard or by contacting us.

Deletion

Request account deletion. We remove personal data unless a legal or transaction record must be retained.

Preferences

Opt out of non-essential analytics or marketing messages without affecting core marketplace access.

05 · Protection

How we protect it

We use encrypted connections, hashed passwords, access controls, monitoring, and audit logs designed to reduce unauthorized access. No online service can promise zero risk, so we also maintain incident response procedures and notify affected users when required.

06 · Questions

Talk to the data team

Send privacy requests to privacy@drophub.com. We aim to acknowledge requests promptly and handle them according to applicable law, including GDPR and Nigeria's data protection framework.
