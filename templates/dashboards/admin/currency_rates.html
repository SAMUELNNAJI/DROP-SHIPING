How these rates are used

Checkout — Paystack (Naira)The NGN amount a buyer is charged is: cart_total_USD × NGN_rate. This is what Paystack receives in kobo.
Checkout — Pi NetworkThe Pi amount a buyer pays is: cart_total_USD × PI_rate. Sent to the Pi SDK or manual wallet.
Boost plan pricingThe ₦ and π equivalents shown on each boost plan card use these rates. A $10 plan at ₦1,600/$ shows as ₦16,000.
Live effect — no restart neededSaving here updates the running process immediately. New checkout pages and boost orders use the new rates within seconds.
