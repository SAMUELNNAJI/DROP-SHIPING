How buyer protection works

Four steps between payment and release — you stay in control the whole way.

Step 1 You pay into escrow Funds are held safely — never sent to the seller yet.
Step 2 Seller ships it You get live tracking from warehouse to doorstep.
Step 3 You inspect it 7 days to confirm the item is exactly as described.
Step 4 We release payment Only after you're happy does the seller get paid.
