Our Origin & Mission

Born from a simple idea: pay your way, trade with zero fear.

In 2022, dropshippers faced the same painful barriers: slow international wire transfers, high payment processor holdbacks, and buyers afraid of cross-border scams. We built DropHub to solve this once and for all.

2022 · THE FRICTION POINT

The Cross-Border Payment Gap

Traditional dropshipping platforms locked out millions of merchants in emerging markets due to rigid banking requirements and 30-day payout delays. Sellers needed local rails and instant crypto settlement.

2024 · THE ESCROW BREAKTHROUGH

Launching Pi Network & Multi-Rail Escrow

DropHub engineered the first 4-step escrow architecture seamlessly accepting Pi Network crypto for instant zero-fee settlements, PayPal for global buyer assurance, and Naira via Paystack for instant local transfers.

2026 · GLOBAL SCALED ECOSYSTEM

50,000+ Stores & 2.4M Orders Protected

Today, DropHub connects verified suppliers across Asia, Europe, and the Americas directly with entrepreneurs across 120+ countries — guaranteeing safety for buyers and instant capital liquidity for sellers.
