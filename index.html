ESCROW PROTECTION

Your Money, Always Safe.

Every transaction on DropHub is protected by our 4-step escrow
system. No more scams, no more chargebacks.

01

Buyer Pays

Buyer completes payment via Pi, PayPal, or Paystack. Funds are immediately captured.

02

DropHub Holds

Payment enters DropHub secure escrow. Neither buyer nor seller can access it yet.

03

Seller Ships

Seller receives order and ships the product. Tracking details are uploaded to the platform.

04

Buyer Confirms

Buyer confirms delivery and approves the transaction. Escrow is released to the seller.
