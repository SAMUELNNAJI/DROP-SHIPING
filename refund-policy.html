The policy

Straightforward, line by line

01

Eligibility

If the item arrives damaged, materially different from the listing, never arrives within the delivery window, or if you request within 14 days of delivery — you are eligible. No forms printed, no threats needed.

02

Escrow clock

Your payment sits in escrow until you confirm delivery. 14 days to open a dispute after delivery, 7 days after the expected window for "late/no-show" claims. The seller is never paid early.

03

How to request

Dashboard → Order → Get Help → choose a reason → add photos. That's it. Our Trust & Safety team reviews within 24h and most decisions land in 48h.

04

Not refundable

Custom/bespoke items already in production, digital goods already delivered, and orders where tracking shows confirmed delivery and your photos show no fault. Restocking is on us otherwise.

05

Partial refunds

For minor issues — a chipped corner, a missing accessory — both sides can agree on a partial refund in escrow. You keep the item; the seller gets a fair portion. Fully handled in-platform.

06

Chargebacks

Escrow means we're on your side before your bank gets involved. If you do open a chargeback, we respond with the escrow evidence within 21 days and most cases close in your favour.

The refund journey

How your money comes home

1 · You open a case

Pick the order, pick the reason, add evidence. Two minutes, done.

2 · Escrow freezes

The seller's payout pauses instantly. They see your case and can reply with evidence too.

3 · We rule

Trust & Safety reviews both sides within 24h–48h. Most cases rule in the buyer's favour automatically.

4 · Money returns

Refunds land back on your Pi wallet instantly, or on PayPal/bank within 1–3 business days.

Questions?

Frequently asked

How long do refunds take to process?

Most refunds are approved within 24–48 hours of opening a case. Once approved, the money returns to your Pi wallet instantly, or to your PayPal/bank account within 1–3 business days.

Can I get a partial refund?

Yes! For minor issues like a chipped corner or missing accessory, you and the seller can agree on a partial refund directly in escrow. You keep the item and get a fair portion of your money back.

What if the seller disagrees with my claim?

Our Trust & Safety team reviews all evidence from both sides and makes a fair decision. Most cases are resolved automatically in the buyer's favour when the evidence is clear.

Are there any items that can't be refunded?

Custom/bespoke items already in production, digital goods already delivered, and orders where tracking shows confirmed delivery with no fault shown in photos are not eligible for refunds.

Ready to start a refund?

Open a case in your dashboard, or talk to the Trust & Safety team — they reply within 4 hours.
