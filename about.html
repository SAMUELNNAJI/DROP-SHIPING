Our Story

Born from a simple idea:
pay your way

In 2022, sellers faced the same frustrating problems: slow international payments, buyers who didn't trust cross-border stores, and tools built for just one country. We knew there had to be a better way.

So DropHub was built around three powerful payment options — Pi Network for instant, fee-free settlements, PayPal for trusted buyer protection, and Naira via Paystack for seamless local transactions. Every order protected by built-in escrow, so buyers and sellers can trade with confidence.
